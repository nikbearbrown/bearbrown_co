# Bay Area AI startup map

Earlier study route: `/startups/bay-area`. The U.S. atlas now lives at `/startups`; see `NATIONAL.md`. The canonical company dataset is `public/data/startups/companies.json`, also served directly as the complete evidence download. The map and CSV export use this same file. Run `node scripts/startups/validate.mjs` before publishing an update.

This first edition is a curated collection of 12 sourced companies, checked 2026-10-31. Ema and Sierra have city and product category corroborated against independent reporting plus company sources. The other ten are explicitly labeled **independent confirmation pending**, in each collapsed card and its evidence panel. Source review is not independent verification. It is not a census, an automated classification benchmark, or an endorsement. Eight task-performing agent products and four builders/infrastructure providers are separate classifications. Website domains identify companies. City reference points are in `lib/startup-map.ts`; they are not company coordinates. All company latitude/longitude fields remain null.

Bear's standing rule: independently corroborate company details across multiple sources, rather than trusting a single source. Multiple company-owned pages are one source family; employer-written YC profiles remain self-reported evidence. Record independent source URLs, claim-level evidence, and verification dates before changing `independent_verification_status` to `city-category-corroborated`. Until then, keep the pending label visible and `independent_verified_at` null. This edition has two records with independently corroborated city and category, and ten pending candidates. The corroboration has a limited scope: it does not validate every company claim, hiring status, or office occupancy. Independent source links, publication dates, summaries, and check dates are in the JSON and evidence panels.

Each record contains a short product excerpt with a source, classification and qualitative confidence, location precision and evidence, activity evidence and observation date, and a review date. Undated open job listings are an explicitly provisional activity signal, not proof of a recently created position. No independent human-label evaluation has been performed; precision and recall are unknown. Product behavior and office occupancy have not been independently tested.

Candidate discovery used https://yc-oss.github.io/api/companies/all.json and https://yc-oss.github.io/api/meta.json; individual company websites, company updates, and employer job boards support the published records. We did not ingest paid databases, Form D, or DataSF. Those sources are possible follow-up work, not implied completed verification.

To update: discover broadly, resolve by domain, review the product against the printed definition, check a resolving website plus activity within 18 months (or clearly describe an observed open role), and inspect location evidence. Preserve uncertainty and source dates. For future precise locations, inspect latest Form D then DataSF then company office/job addresses, reconcile conflicts, validate occupancy and geocoding, and distinguish registered agents or home addresses. A city-only job posting stays `city`, never `office`. Do not add invented pins or jitter. Never include pending candidates in confirmed-company totals; candidate totals must stay explicitly labeled.

`bay-land.json` is public-domain Natural Earth 1:10m land, clipped to longitude −123.05…−121.55 and latitude 37.05…38.35, simplified with a 0.0006° tolerance, and rewound clockwise for D3 spherical polygons. Original: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_land.geojson. License: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/. This is a generalized coast, not a parcel or street basemap. City reference points are approximate geographic anchors; the map does not claim municipal boundary precision.

The Mercator scale bar derives distance from the inverse projection using D3 spherical distance at its displayed latitude, updating with zoom/pan. Bubble area is proportional to count; pie sectors show classification mix. Search/category/layer affect map counts; city selection further narrows the directory/statistics and dims other cities. SVG controls have keyboard equivalents and the HTML city buttons provide a second way to filter. All company evidence is HTML, not tooltip-only content.
